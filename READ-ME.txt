AFRICAN PRIDE WEBSITE  (multi-page, no backend)

OPEN IT
  Double-click index.html. Works offline. Pictures and fonts are inside the folder,
  so keep the whole folder together (or share the whole zip).

PAGES
  index.html     Home        shop.html      Shop (filters + search)
  product.html   One page per piece (opened as product.html?id=collar-tee)
  story.html     Our Story + Lookbook      contact.html   How ordering works
  checkout.html  Details + order summary, sends the order to WhatsApp

WHERE THINGS ARE
  css/    one file per job: base, nav, cart, hero, products, sections, pages, responsive, animations
  js/     data/settings.js  <- YOUR WHATSAPP NUMBER, currency, delivery fee, pickup address
          data/products.js  <- products, prices, colours (add soldOut:true for SOLD OUT)
          layout.js (header/footer/cart drawer, shared by all pages), cart.js, products.js,
          nav.js, slideshow.js, animations.js, main.js, pages/ (one small file per page)
  images/ photos + logo      fonts/ bundled fonts

FIRST THING TO DO
  Open js/data/settings.js and fill in:  whatsapp: '2207001234'   (your number, country code, no +)
  Until you do, Checkout says the WhatsApp number is not set.

PUT IT ONLINE (free): drag this whole folder into Netlify Drop (app.netlify.com/drop)
or upload it to GitHub Pages / Cloudflare Pages. You get a link to share on WhatsApp.
