The Design

More Than a Graphic.
A Legacy.

The African Pride silhouette tells a story older than borders. A mother grinding grain with mortar and pestle — the daily rhythm of sustenance and survival. Her baby wrapped securely on her back, carried forward through every generation. Her headscarf flows into the outline of the African continent itself, a reminder that heritage is not worn lightly — it is lived, passed down, and honored.

The cursive "Ray" signature on the chest is a mark of authenticity. Every tee is a canvas for identity — minimal on the surface, monumental in meaning.
